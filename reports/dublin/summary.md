**Dublin 2040 Grid Capacity Plan (Python, Monte Carlo):** bottom-up model of County Dublin's electricity peak to 2040 from CSO consumption and Census 2022 data for 322 electoral divisions (heat pumps, EVs, housing, business): central peak +115% to 2,414 MW; mapped new load onto 113 ESB Networks substations (77 run short); sized ~24 new stations and €379–817m station investment, and showed flexibility (smart EV charging, demand response) saves ~€203m.
