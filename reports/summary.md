**Cork 2040 Grid Capacity Plan (Python, Monte Carlo):** bottom-up model of Co. Cork's electricity peak to 2040 from CSO consumption and Census 2022 data for 391 electoral divisions (heat pumps, EVs, housing, business): central peak +91% to 1,110 MW; mapped new load onto 65 ESB Networks substations (45 run short); sized ~10 new stations and €194–346m station investment, and showed flexibility (smart EV charging, demand response) saves ~€68m.
